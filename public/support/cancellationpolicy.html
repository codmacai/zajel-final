Cancellations & Refunds Rules

The following rules apply from the moment the booking is paid for & confirmed.

The time the cancellation is submitted through the app determines which rule is applied.

After a Successful Pickup

Once the package has been collected, the booking can no longer be cancelled. If delivery fails, the following applies:

ScenarioCharge / RefundWhat Happens Next
Recipient is unavailable to accept delivery The booking fee is non-refundable in this case. One additional delivery attempt will be made at no extra charge. If the second attempt also fails, the package is returned to the sender within 7 business days.
Delivery could not be completed due to an address issue 50% fee applies. Remaining balance is refunded. One additional delivery attempt will be made at no extra charge. If the second attempt also fails, the package is returned to the sender within 7 business days.
Delivery incomplete due to an operational issue Full refund if redelivery is not possible. Priority delivery shall be arranged at no extra cost.

Note: The above applies to both Same Day and Next Day services.

International Shipments

This section explains cancellations and refunds for international shipments booked through the Zajel app.

Cancellation before shipment collection

If you cancel an international shipment before it has been collected, you will receive a full refund of the amount paid. No pickup or other charges will be deducted.

Cancellation after collection – shipment still at origin

If your shipment has already been collected but has not yet moved from the origin location, you may request a cancellation. In this case, a pickup charge of AED 26.25 will be deducted and the remaining amount refunded to you.

Shipments that cannot move from origin

If your international shipment cannot proceed from the origin location for any of the reasons below, a pickup charge of AED 26.25 will apply and the remaining balance will be refunded to you:

  • Incorrect or incomplete shipment details
  • Customs-related issues
  • Prohibited or restricted items
  • Missing required documentation
  • Any other issue preventing the shipment from proceeding

Undeliverable shipments at destination

If your shipment reaches the destination country but cannot be delivered to the recipient for any of the reasons below, no refund will be provided, and any additional applicable charges will be payable by you as the sender:

  • The recipient refuses to accept the shipment
  • The recipient declines to pay applicable customs duties, taxes, or import charges
  • The recipient is unreachable, or the address provided is incorrect, incomplete, or insufficient for delivery
  • The recipient does not provide the required clearance documents
  • The recipient does not respond to delivery attempts, collect, or receive the shipment within the carrier’s designated holding period
  • Any other issue preventing collection or delivery

In these cases, the following additional charges may apply. They are non-refundable and are the responsibility of the sender:

  • Return-to-origin (RTO) or destruction charges
  • Customs duties, taxes, clearance fees, storage charges, and any other destination-related charges, where applicable

Refunds

  • All refunds are returned to the original payment method.
  • Refunds are processed up to 10 business days after your cancellation is confirmed.
  • Processing times may vary depending on the bank or payment provider.
